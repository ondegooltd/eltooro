# ORDER PROCESS (HOW ORDERS ARE MADE)

## Cart and Product Features
- Customer adds item to cart and Proceeds to Checkout
  - A buy button should be in each product card
  - An add to cart button should display when the cursor is hovered over the product image
- Product card should have an increase(+) or decrease (-) functionality to give customers the ability to add more products or reduce the number of items/products they want to buy.

## Product Page
- Product page should contain product Description
- Beneath the description, there should be a section for related products.

## Checkout Page
- It should contain the basic checkout information, including:
  - Name of the product
  - Price
  - Quantity
  - Service fee
  - Subtotal
- Checkout page should also contain billing information/details or shipping information.

## Service Charge
- 3ghc per item for the Ghanaian market
- 30ghc for the international customers

## Payment
- Payment before delivery
- Payment should be made with either:
  - MoMo (for Ghanaian customers)
  - Any supported Paystack international payment mediums (For international clients)
- After successful payment, customer should receive an SMS or email

## Delivery Time
- **Delivery within Winneba:** 4hr - 24 hours
- **Delivery within Accra and central region:** 6hrs to 24hrs
- **Delivery outside Accra and central region:** 2 - 5 days
- **Delivery outside Ghana:** 6-8 weeks by ship
